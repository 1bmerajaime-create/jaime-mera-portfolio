About me

Jaime Mera

I’m a Product Design Lead at AILY Labs in Madrid. I make complex products simple — from design systems serving millions to mobile apps used globally. I work across the entire process from research to delivery. I don’t chase pretty screens — I solve business problems that happen to need beautiful interfaces.

Experience

I lead the design strategy for Aily, a mobile-first AI decision platform for pharma. I translate complex enterprise workflows into consumer-grade interfaces that prioritize decisions over data, applying Decision Driven Design across 9+ modules (Finance, R&D, GTM, Manufacturing, etc.). I balance rapid delivery with systematic consolidation—ensuring templates, components, and patterns scale.

As a senior product designer my main roles are:

  • Leading the design process from ideation to execution, working closely with cross-functional teams such as product managers, engineers, and other designers.
  • Conducting user research and gathering insights to inform design decisions. Collaborating with product managers to define user needs and create user personas.
  • Creating design concepts and iterating on them based on user feedback and business requirements. Designing user interfaces that are intuitive, easy to use, and visually appealing. Ensuring that the user experience is consistent across touchpoints, including mobile, web, and desktop.
  • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure that the design aligns with the product vision and business goals. Also collaborating with engineers to ensure that the design is feasible and can be implemented efficiently.

As a Product Designer my most relevant tasks are to:

  • Define interactions and services based on relevant business, technical, and design considerations.
  • Make design ideas tangible for the Fjord team and the client through visual storytelling and rapid prototyping.
  • Explain and discuss design decisions eloquently, and give design feedback.
  • Contribute to definition and execution of visual design language throughout engagements at the project level.

As part of Accenture Interactive, a performance unit focused in digital transformation and digital marketing, in which we create and design customer centric services, content strategies and hybrid spaces always with a layer of user experience (UX). Focused on a experience architecture, building a digital strategy for important firms in health, public, energy, banking and other key sectors that want to transform the way its business works, making it more digital, customer-centric and results-oriented always using a service design methodology.

My day to day tasks are:
